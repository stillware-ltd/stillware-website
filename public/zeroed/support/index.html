Zeroed Support

Zeroed is a local-first budgeting app by Stillware Ltd. Your financial data lives only on your own devices, encrypted with a password you choose — there is no account and we operate no servers holding your data.

Contact us

For help, questions, or feedback, email support@stillwareltd.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

I forgot my budget password. Your budget is encrypted with a key derived from your password, and nobody — including us — can recover it without the password. This is a deliberate privacy guarantee; please keep your password safe.

How do I use Zeroed on another device? Enable Google Drive sync in Settings on your first device, then connect the same Google account on the new one. Your encrypted budget and your lifetime license travel with it.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the upgrade screen and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the store account that made the purchase.
