## Puzzle 1: Mate in two (beginner, rated 991)

![Puzzle 1: White to play after Bxc2](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/puzzle-1.webp)

**White to play.** Black has just played bishop takes on c2. It wins a rook, or so it seems. The black king on h8 is short of squares. The king is running out of squares. Can you find the winning check?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/01xnx](https://lichess.org/training/01xnx). It was rated 991 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Rf8+** — White: Rook to f8, check.
2. **Kh7** — Black: King to h7.
3. **Qg8#** — White: Queen to g8, checkmate.

Rook to f8, check. The king cannot go to g8, because our queen on b3 controls that square. Black has no choice. King to h7. And now, queen to g8, checkmate.

![Puzzle 1 final position after Qg8#](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/solution-1.webp)

**The pattern.** A mate in two. The first move takes away the escape, the second delivers mate, and the reply in between is forced.

## Puzzle 2: Trapped piece (intermediate, rated 1698)

![Puzzle 2: White to play after Ng6](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/puzzle-2.webp)

**White to play.** Black just played knight to g6. And the black bishop on h5 has nothing defending it. One of Black's pieces has run out of squares. Can you find the move that wins it?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/01ctt](https://lichess.org/training/01ctt). It was rated 1698 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Bxd6+** — White: Bishop takes on d6, check.
2. **Kxd6** — Black: King takes on d6.
3. **g4** — White: Pawn to g4.
4. **Nf4** — Black: Knight to f4.
5. **gxh5** — White: Pawn takes on h5.

Bishop takes on d6, check. The bishops come off. Black steps out of check. King takes on d6. Pawn to g4. Black plays knight to f4. Pawn takes on h5. That wins the bishop for our pawn.

![Puzzle 2 final position after gxh5](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/solution-2.webp)

**The pattern.** A trapped piece. It has nowhere safe to go, and it falls.

## Puzzle 3: Queen fork (advanced, rated 2072)

![Puzzle 3: White to play after Qh7](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/puzzle-3.webp)

**White to play.** Black just played queen to h7. And the black bishop on b5 has nothing defending it. Where can our queen land to attack both at once?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/02ZU4](https://lichess.org/training/02ZU4). It was rated 2072 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Qb4** — White: Queen to b4.
2. **Qe4** — Black: Queen to e4.
3. **Qxb5** — White: Queen takes on b5.

Queen to b4. The queen attacks the bishop on f4 and the bishop on b5 at the same time. Black plays queen to e4. Queen takes on b5. That wins the bishop.

![Puzzle 3 final position after Qxb5](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/solution-3.webp)

**The pattern.** A queen fork. The queen attacks two undefended targets at once, often with check, and only one of them can be saved.

## Puzzle 4: Quiet move (master, rated 2661)

![Puzzle 4: Black to play after Kd1](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/puzzle-4.webp)

**Black to play.** White just played king to d1. It looks safe. It is not. No check and no capture, but one move wins. Can you find it?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/0HECK](https://lichess.org/training/0HECK). It was rated 2661 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Kb2** — Black: King to b2.
2. **Na2** — White: Knight to a2.
3. **Bb7** — Black: Bishop to b7.
4. **Nxc3** — White: Knight takes on c3.
5. **Kxc3** — Black: King takes on c3.

King to b2. White defends with knight to a2. Bishop to b7. White takes. Knight takes on c3. King takes on c3. That wins the knight.

![Puzzle 4 final position after Kxc3](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-32-mate-in-two-trapped-piece-queen-fork-quiet-move/solution-4.webp)

**The pattern.** A quiet move. No check, no capture, and yet it is the strongest move on the board because of the threat it creates.
