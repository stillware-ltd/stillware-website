## Puzzle 1: Pawn fork (beginner, rated 892)

![Puzzle 1: White to play after Kf5](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/puzzle-1.webp)

**White to play.** Black has just played king to f5. And the black rook on d5 has nothing defending it. Can you see the square that attacks two pieces at the same time?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/00itj](https://lichess.org/training/00itj). It was rated 892 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **e4+** — White: Pawn to e4, check.
2. **Ke6** — Black: King to e6.
3. **exd5+** — White: Pawn takes on d5, check.

Pawn to e4, check. The pawn attacks the king on f5 and the rook on d5 at the same time. The king must move. King to e6. Pawn takes on d5, check. The king cannot go to d6, because our bishop on c7 controls that square.

![Puzzle 1 final position after exd5+](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/solution-1.webp)

**The pattern.** We win the rook for a pawn. A pawn fork. The humblest piece attacks two bigger ones at once, and whichever moves, the other falls.

## Puzzle 2: Bishop fork (intermediate, rated 1428)

![Puzzle 2: White to play after Bb2](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/puzzle-2.webp)

**White to play.** Black has just played bishop to b2. Take a moment to look at the whole board. Would you give up your rook here? There is a reason to.

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/006wz](https://lichess.org/training/006wz). It was rated 1428 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Rxb2** — White: Rook takes on b2.
2. **Rxb2** — Black: Rook takes on b2.
3. **Bd4+** — White: Bishop to d4, check.
4. **f6** — Black: Pawn to f6.
5. **Bxb2** — White: Bishop takes on b2.

Rook takes on b2. We give up the rook for a bishop. Black takes. Rook takes on b2. Bishop to d4, check. The bishop attacks the king on g7 and the rook on b2 at the same time. Black plays pawn to f6. Bishop takes on b2. That wins the rook.

![Puzzle 2 final position after Bxb2](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/solution-2.webp)

**The pattern.** A bishop fork. Two targets on crossing diagonals, one bishop between them.

## Puzzle 3: Trapped piece (advanced, rated 2004)

![Puzzle 3: Black to play after Rb2](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/puzzle-3.webp)

**Black to play.** White has just played rook to b2. And the white knight on e7 has nothing defending it. One of White's pieces has run out of squares. Can you find the move that wins it?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/025gx](https://lichess.org/training/025gx). It was rated 2004 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Qe8** — Black: Queen to e8.
2. **Nd5** — White: Knight to d5.
3. **exd5** — Black: Pawn takes on d5.

Queen to e8. White tries knight to d5. Pawn takes on d5. That wins the knight for our pawn.

![Puzzle 3 final position after exd5](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/solution-3.webp)

**The pattern.** A trapped piece. It has nowhere safe to go, and it falls.

## Puzzle 4: Clearance sacrifice (master, rated 2657)

![Puzzle 4: Black to play after Bxh6](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/puzzle-4.webp)

**Black to play.** White has just played bishop takes on h6. It wins a pawn, or so it seems. Notice the white king on g1 and the white queen on f3, close together. Which capture wins material?

Play it on Lichess before reading on: [lichess.org/training/06z4K](https://lichess.org/training/06z4K). It was rated 2657 in the Lichess database we took it from; ratings there shift a little as people solve it.

### Solution

1. **Rxf3** — Black: Rook takes on f3.
2. **gxf3** — White: Pawn takes on f3.
3. **Rd8** — Black: Rook to d8.

Rook takes on f3. That wins the queen for our rook. White takes back. Pawn takes on f3. Rook to d8.

![Puzzle 4 final position after Rd8](/blog/images/daily-chess-puzzle-33-pawn-fork-bishop-fork-trapped-piece-clearance-sacrifice/solution-4.webp)

**The pattern.** We come out a piece ahead. A clearance sacrifice. If you were tempted by pawn takes on h6, White answers rook takes on h6, check and the chance is gone.
