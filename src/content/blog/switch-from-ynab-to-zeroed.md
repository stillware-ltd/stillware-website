So you've decided to stop paying $109 a year to budget your own money. Good news: because YNAB and Zeroed both use the same zero-based envelope method, switching is mostly a data move, not a re-learning exercise. Every skill you built in YNAB — giving dollars jobs, rolling with the punches, ageing your money — transfers directly.

Here's the whole migration, start to finish. Most people are done inside an hour.

## Before you start

- **Install Zeroed** on [Windows or Android](/zeroed) and start the free trial — it's 34 days, fully unlocked, no card, no account. You can run YNAB and Zeroed side by side for the entire switch, so nothing is at risk.
- **Don't cancel YNAB yet.** Do that at the end, once you've confirmed everything came across. (If your renewal date is close, YNAB keeps working until the paid period ends.)

## Step 1: Export your data from YNAB

1. Open YNAB in a **browser** (the export lives in the web app, not mobile).
2. Click your **budget name** (top-left) to open the budget menu.
3. Choose **Export budget data**.
4. YNAB downloads a **.zip** containing two CSV files: a **Register** file (every transaction you've ever entered) and a **Plan/Budget** file (your monthly category allocations).
5. Unzip it somewhere you can find it. The Register CSV is the one that matters.

That file is your complete financial history — payees, categories, amounts, dates, account names, memos. It's yours, and it's about to outlive your subscription.

## Step 2: Import your history into Zeroed

1. In Zeroed, create your accounts first (current/checking, savings, credit cards — same names you used in YNAB keeps things tidy).
2. Go to **Import** and choose the Register CSV.
3. Zeroed's universal parser reads the file and shows you a preview — check that dates, amounts, and payees look right, and adjust the column mapping if your regional format needs it.
4. Run the import. **Duplicate detection** is automatic, so if you ever import an overlapping bank statement later, you won't get double entries.

Prefer a lighter start? Some switchers import only the last 3–6 months and let the deep history stay in the archived CSV. Both approaches work — Zeroed handles multi-year registers fine, so it's taste, not a technical limit.

## Step 3: Rebuild your category plan

The Register import brings your transactions and their category names. Now recreate your envelope structure:

1. Set up your **category groups and categories** in Zeroed to mirror your YNAB plan (have the Plan CSV open for reference — it lists every category and what you assigned monthly).
2. This is also the moment YNAB veterans rarely get: a **guilt-free fresh start**. Ten years of category archaeology ("Misc II (old) (don't use)") doesn't have to come with you. Keep what worked, drop what didn't.
3. Enter your current account balances or reconcile against your latest bank statement, then assign your available money to categories — the familiar move: give every dollar a job.

## Step 4: Set up the things YNAB did in the background

- **Recurring bills:** add your scheduled transactions (rent, utilities, subscriptions you actually want to keep) so upcoming bills show on the Today view.
- **Statement imports going forward:** instead of bank sync, you'll import a CSV/PDF/OFX statement periodically — most banks export these from online banking in two clicks. With saved import rules, a monthly import takes a few minutes, and reconciliation catches anything a broken bank feed would have silently missed.
- **Sync (optional):** if you use Zeroed on phone + computer, connect your own Google Drive. Your data is encrypted before upload; no Zeroed servers are involved because none exist.
- **Lock it:** turn on biometric unlock.

## Step 5: Run parallel for a few weeks, then cancel

Keep YNAB read-only while you live in Zeroed for a pay cycle or two. When your numbers reconcile and the habit has moved, cancel the YNAB subscription and enjoy the strangest part of switching: that's the last budgeting bill you'll ever budget for. ## What's different day-to-day (honest edition)

- **No automatic bank sync.** You import statements instead. Some people find this a downgrade; many find the added awareness is the point of budgeting. Decide for yourself during the trial.
- **No web app.** Zeroed is installed software — your budget doesn't live in a browser tab or on anyone's server.
- **No shared cloud budget.** Partners share via a synced Drive folder or a shared device rather than separate logins.
